# Design System: TaskBoard

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "One Board, Three Skins"**

TaskBoard is one task manager wearing three complete, user-selectable skins. The board itself never changes: the same tasks, filters, composer, states, ES/EN language switch, theme toggle and accessible structure come from one shared behaviour layer, and each skin re-draws all of it in its own world. Comic is the default; the visitor switches with the style-switch row under the top bar of every skin, and the choice is remembered in this browser. A skin is total: it owns the page ground, the body face, selection, caret, focus ring, scrollbar and the browser's theme colour, so no surface ever shows a half-switched mix.

The three worlds are deliberately far apart. **Comic, "The Printed Comic Page"**, prints the board as a four-colour comic page with inked panels and stepped animation. **Brutal, "The Pressable Slab Board"**, builds it from heavy Nightfall-outlined slabs on a Tropical Pink ground that lift under the cursor and sink when pressed. **Classic, "The Original Soft Board"**, is the original TaskBoard restored as-is: Inter, zinc neutrals, a violet accent and soft translucent cards. Each has light and dark editions and its own depth system; none borrows another's.

**Comic (default).** TaskBoard's Comic skin is a page of a comic book that happens to be a task manager. Every surface is a panel drawn in 2D that fakes 3D: white paper, a thick near-black ink outline, a stepped ink extrusion to the lower right, and a Ben-Day halftone shadow cast behind it. The page sits over a parallax sky: a cyan dotted sky by day, a violet night with magenta dots after dark, with speed lines, a halftone sun or moon, and two layers of inked skyline drifting at different depths with the pointer and scroll. Everything is CSS and inline SVG; the skin ships no raster art. The palette is four-colour process print: cyan, magenta, yellow and ink on paper. Display type is hand-lettered comic (Bangers) with cyan and magenta plates knocked out of register; working text is Archivo, a variable grotesque whose width axis condenses labels the way a letterer would. Motion is animated "on twos": scripted transitions advance in stepped frames instead of smooth easing, so the page moves like drawn animation. Completing a task triggers the signature onomatopoeia burst. The style is inspired by the comic art direction of *Into the Spider-Verse* and stops at the art style. The skin carries no characters, logos, or franchise imagery. Personality never gets in the way of the task: titles stay plain Archivo, controls stay obvious, and every decorative motion turns off under reduced motion.

**Brutal.** Kinetic Neo-Brutalism in the user-pinned "Ponche Tropical" palette. Every card and button is a warm-white slab with a 4px Nightfall border and a 6px zero-blur Nightfall shadow; under a real pointer it lifts diagonally with its shadow growing to 10px, and pressing sinks it fully onto the page. Type is Archivo at weight 900 stretched wide for shouts and plain bold Archivo for content. The header is a bento grid, the board's live counts ride two full-bleed, tilted Liquid Sunset marquees that accelerate with scroll, a Heatwave stamp pops when a task is finished, and on wide screens hand-drawn SVG stickers sit in the margins to be dragged around. Night re-grounds the board on Nightfall while the accents stay put.

**Classic.** The original TaskBoard design restored at the user's request: a near-white or near-black canvas with a faint masked grid and slowly breathing violet, indigo, sky and fuchsia halos, translucent white cards with hairline zinc borders and a backdrop blur, violet-to-indigo gradient accents on the logo tile, add button and progress ring, emerald completion checks, and a waving 👋 after the greeting. It is kept faithful on purpose; its only changes are accessibility and fit fixes (listed under Components), not restyling.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Three total skins (Comic default, Brutal, Classic) over one shared behaviour layer; switching re-draws everything, never mixes.
- Each skin owns its page ground, body face, selection, caret, focus ring, scrollbar and browser theme colour.
- Comic: inked panels with ink extrusion plus halftone cast shadow as the only depth system.
- Comic: four-colour process palette; yellow narrator captions; magenta as the "done/active" plate.
- Comic: Bangers lettering with cyan/magenta misregistration for display and SFX only; Archivo for everything you read.
- Comic: stepped "on twos" timing for scripted motion; springs only for direct hover and tap response.
- Comic: slight hand-pasted tilt on panels; small radii (2-4px) so panels read as cut paper, not cards.
- Comic: day and night are two full printings of the same page, not a dimmed copy.
- Brutal: Nightfall 4px borders and 0-blur offset shadows; tactile lift-and-sink press on a pinned overshoot curve.
- Brutal: wide Archivo 900 display, Liquid Sunset marquees, Paradise secondary cards and tickets, Heatwave calls to action.
- Classic: the restored original, soft translucent cards, zinc plus violet, Inter, light motion.

## Colors

Shared mechanics: the active skin is set as `data-skin` on the root element (Comic when nothing is stored), and the skin's page ground, page text, selection, caret, focus colour and width, and scrollbar colours switch with it. The browser theme colour follows the skin and theme: Comic sky-ground / night-ground, Brutal brutal-ground / brutal-nightfall, Classic classic-violet-deep in both themes.

### Comic

A four-colour print run: process cyan, magenta and yellow plus ink on paper, re-inked for a night edition.

#### Primary
- **Process Yellow** (process-yellow): the action and narration colour. The primary button, narrator captions (date box, waking notice, footer stack line), the logo tile, hover fill on secondary controls, the edit field, and text selection. Text on yellow is always fixed ink (#14101f) in both themes.

#### Secondary
- **Process Magenta** (process-magenta, process-magenta-night): the "done and active" plate. Checked checkboxes, the active filter plate, active language and skin, the progress meter fill, strike-through on completed titles, caret and accent colour, and the right-hand misregistered plate on lettering. In the night edition it also becomes the extrusion colour (extrude-night) and the dot colour of the sky.

#### Tertiary
- **Process Cyan** (process-cyan, process-cyan-night): the left-hand misregistered plate on lettering and the burst, hover fill for the edit action, and the empty-state icon tile. Also the night edition's cast-shadow dot colour.

#### Neutral
- **Sky Ground** (sky-ground, sky-ground-deep): the day page behind the panels, overlaid with white Ben-Day dots (rgb(255 255 255 / 0.32)).
- **Night Ground** (night-ground, night-ground-deep): the night page, overlaid with magenta dots (rgb(224 19 122 / 0.28)).
- **Panel Paper** (panel-paper, panel-paper-night): the fill of every inked panel. Completed tasks drop to panel-paper-done with a faint grey halftone over it.
- **Ink** (ink, ink-night): every outline, extrusion (by day), stroke on lettering, checkbox and control border. By night, readable text switches to ink-text-night while outlines stay near-black.
- **Soft Ink** (ink-soft, ink-soft-night): metadata lines, placeholders, the "/total" in the meter, toast descriptions.
- **Danger** (danger, danger-night): delete hover, the error speech balloon, the error state's extrusion and icon tile, the near-limit character count.
- **Skyline** (far-city, near-city, their night pair, lit-window): background-only colours for the two parallax city layers and their windows (yellow by day, magenta by night).

#### Named Rules
**The Process Plate Rule.** In Comic, colour comes from the four process plates and the paper. A new accent needs a print reason; there is no fifth hue in the skin.

**The Fixed Ink On Yellow Rule.** Anything set on Process Yellow uses fixed ink (#14101f) regardless of theme. Yellow never flips, so its text never flips.

**The Two Printings Rule.** Night is a second print run: the ground goes violet, the extrusion goes magenta, the cast dots go cyan, the windows go magenta. Never build dark mode by dimming the day palette.

### Brutal

"Ponche Tropical", user-pinned: one dark line colour, a warm pink ground, warm white surfaces, and three accents with fixed jobs.

#### Primary
- **Heatwave** (brutal-heat): the call to action and the hot signal. The add and retry buttons, the floating pending badge, the completion stamp, checked checkboxes, the progress fill, the reactive cursor, the active filter's count chip, strike-through on finished titles, the near-limit count, the composer's error border and error balloon, selection and caret, the logo tile and the marquee sparks.

#### Secondary
- **Paradise** (brutal-para): the secondary plate. The progress bento card, finished task slabs, the active filter and active ES/EN and skin plates, tickets (status, footer stack line) and the metadata pills on pending tasks. Text on Paradise is always warm white (brutal-paper).

#### Tertiary
- **Liquid Sunset** (brutal-sun): light and motion. Both marquees, the greeting card's top strip, state-card header strips (waking), the inline edit field, and the hover flood on every secondary control.

#### Neutral
- **Tropical Pink** (brutal-ground): the day ground.
- **Warm White** (brutal-paper): every slab by day, text on Paradise, and the finished-task metadata pill.
- **Nightfall** (brutal-nightfall): every border, every shadow and the main text by day; it is also the night ground, and the fixed text colour on Heatwave and Liquid Sunset in both themes.
- **Nightfall Soft** (brutal-text-soft, brutal-text-soft-night): summaries, placeholders, explanations and toast descriptions.
- **Night Slab** (brutal-paper-night) and **Night Line** (brutal-ink-night): the night edition's slab fill and its borders and shadows; night text is warm white.
- **Online Mint** (brutal-online): the status dot when the API is online, the only hue outside the palette.

#### Named Rules
**The Nightfall Line Rule.** Every border, shadow and outline in Brutal is the one line colour (Nightfall by day, Night Line by night). Lines never take an accent.

**The Fixed Nightfall On Accent Rule.** Text and icons on Heatwave and Liquid Sunset are Nightfall (#351c4d) in both themes; text on Paradise is warm white. Accents never flip, so what sits on them never flips.

**The Re-Grounded Night Rule.** Night moves the ground to Nightfall and the slabs to Night Slab; Heatwave, Liquid Sunset and Paradise stay exactly as they are.

### Classic

The original zinc-and-violet palette, unchanged except for secondary text raised to zinc-500.

#### Primary
- **Original Violet** (classic-violet, classic-violet-deep, classic-violet-night): the accent. The violet-to-indigo gradient on the logo tile, add button and progress ring, the date in the subline, composer focus border and plus icon, checkbox hover border, edit-field ring, footer link hover, caret (classic-violet-deep), focus ring (classic-violet, 2px) and the 25% violet selection.
- **Indigo** (classic-indigo): the far end of every violet gradient.

#### Secondary
- **Emerald** (classic-emerald to classic-emerald-deep): the gradient fill of a checked checkbox and the online status dot.

#### Neutral
- **Canvas** (classic-canvas, classic-canvas-night): the page ground; cards are white at 80% (pending) or 40% (done) over it, zinc-900 at 60% by night.
- **Zinc Ink** (classic-ink, classic-ink-night): headings, task titles and page text.
- **Zinc Secondary** (classic-secondary, classic-secondary-night): metadata, the subline, placeholders, inactive tabs, footer and the list hint.
- **Hairline** (classic-border): card, composer, pill and filter-group borders (white at 10% by night).
- **Rose** (classic-rose, classic-rose-deep): delete hover, composer error border and message, the error state and the near-limit count.
- **Amber** (classic-amber): the waking-server notice and status dot.

#### Named Rules
**The Restored Original Rule.** Classic reproduces the original design as the user asked. Its violet gradients, blur halos, translucent cards and 👋 belong to Classic only and are not a pattern for Comic, Brutal or any new surface.

## Typography

Shared mechanics: the body face is a per-skin variable, Archivo Variable for Comic and Brutal, Inter Variable (with cv11 and ss01) for Classic. All faces are self-hosted.

### Comic

**Display Font:** Bangers (with Archivo Variable, ui-sans-serif fallback), self-hosted via @fontsource
**Body Font:** Archivo Variable with the wdth axis (with ui-sans-serif, system-ui, Segoe UI fallback), self-hosted via @fontsource-variable

**Character:** Bangers is the letterer's hand, loud, slanted and all-caps, used for what the page shouts. Archivo is the typesetter, a sturdy grotesque whose width axis (78-100%) condenses labels into tight comic-caption caps without changing family.

#### Hierarchy
- **Display** (Bangers 400, clamp(2.75rem, 10vw, 4.75rem), line-height 0.95): the greeting headline, set as lettering (paper-white fill, 2px ink stroke, 4px stepped ink extrusion, cyan plate offset -3px -1px, magenta plate offset 7px 5px). Error SFX uses the same treatment at 3rem; the logotype uses it at 1.55-1.9rem with a 1.5-2px stroke.
- **Headline** (Bangers 400, 1.875-2.25rem, line-height 1): empty-state titles and the meter count. Plain ink, without plates.
- **Title** (Archivo 600, 1.125rem, line-height 1.375): task titles and composer text. Always Archivo so user content stays readable; wraps anywhere.
- **Body** (Archivo 500-700, 0.875-1rem): state explanations, caption summaries, toast text.
- **Label** (Archivo 800, 0.75rem, letter-spacing 0.025em, uppercase, wdth 85%; 78% on narrow viewports): metadata lines, filter tabs, status text, meter caption, list hint, the skin-switch label.
- **Button lettering** (Bangers 400, 1.125-1.25rem, letter-spacing 0.05em): the primary yellow buttons, the ES/EN and skin switches, plus counts in filter chips.

#### Named Rules
**The Lettering Is Shouted Rule.** Bangers carries only shouts: greetings, SFX, button calls, counts and empty-state titles. User content and anything read twice is Archivo.

**The Width Not Size Rule.** To fit a label, condense its width axis (100% to 85% to 78%) before shrinking it below 0.75rem.

### Brutal

**Display Font:** Archivo Variable at weight 900, wdth 118%, tracking -0.02em, uppercase
**Body Font:** Archivo Variable (with ui-sans-serif, system-ui, Segoe UI fallback)

**Character:** One family pushed to its extremes: the display is the same grotesque at full black weight and widened past normal, so headings read as stamped slabs; working text stays plain bold Archivo.

#### Hierarchy
- **Display** (900, wdth 118%, clamp(2.3rem, 8.5vw, 3.6rem), line-height 0.92, uppercase, balanced): the greeting in the bento card, punctuation stripped.
- **Numeral** (900, wdth 118%, 3rem, line-height 1, tabular): the done/total count in the progress card; 1.875rem in the pending badge.
- **Headline** (900, wdth 118%, 1.25-1.5rem, uppercase): marquee phrases, empty-state titles, the wordmark (1.05rem at wdth 100% on narrow phones, 1.125rem from 380px, 1.5rem at wdth 118% from 640px) and the completion stamp at 0.875rem.
- **Title** (700, 1.125rem, line-height 1.375): task titles, composer text and the edit field.
- **Body** (600, 0.875-1rem): summaries, state explanations, toast descriptions.
- **Button** (900, 1rem, uppercase, wdth 110-112%): the add and retry calls.

## Elevation & Depth

Each skin has its own depth system and never borrows another's: Comic prints depth, Brutal stacks it with hard offsets, Classic lifts with soft shadow and blur.

### Comic

Depth is printed, not lit. There are no blurred shadows anywhere on the comic page. Every raised surface is an inked panel: a 3px ink border, a stepped extrusion built from stacked 0-blur offsets up to the panel's depth (default 5px), and behind it a Ben-Day halftone cast shadow (6px dot grid) offset a further 6px right and 7px down. Depth is a variable, so state is expressed by changing how far the panel stands off the page: it rises on hover or focus and flattens when the task is done.

#### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Ink extrusion** (`box-shadow: 1px 1px 0 var(--extrude), 2px 2px 0 var(--extrude), 3px 3px 0 var(--extrude), calc(var(--depth) - 1px) calc(var(--depth) - 1px) 0 var(--extrude), var(--depth) var(--depth) 0 var(--extrude)`): the body of every inked panel. Depth 5px default, 8px task hover, 7px composer focus, 3px for top-bar controls, the skin switch, filter group and footer links, 2px for completed tasks.
- **Halftone cast** (`background-image: radial-gradient(var(--cast) 34%, transparent 38%); background-size: 6px 6px; transform: translate(calc(var(--depth) + 6px), calc(var(--depth) + 7px))`): the dotted shadow behind every inked panel; ink at 55% by day, cyan at 45% by night.
- **Ink offset** (`box-shadow: 3px 3px 0 var(--ink)`, hover `5px 5px 0 var(--ink)`): the flat ink drop under yellow buttons, narrator captions and icon tiles that sit inside a panel. Toasts use 4px 4px 0 var(--extrude).
- **Lettering extrusion** (`text-shadow: 1px..4px stepped ink, -3px -1px 0 var(--cyan), 7px 5px 0 var(--magenta)`): depth and misregistration for display lettering only.

#### Named Rules
**The Printed Depth Rule.** On the comic page, depth is ink extrusion plus halftone cast, never a blur. If a shadow has a blur radius, it does not belong on this page.

**The Depth Is State Rule.** A panel's distance from the page is its state: raised on hover or focus, flattened when done. Change --depth; don't add a new shadow.

### Brutal

Depth is a hard Nightfall offset with zero blur, and state is physical position. A slab rests 6px off the page; under a real pointer it lifts toward the light (moves up-left 4px while its shadow grows to 10px); pressed, it sinks onto the page (moves down-right 6px, shadow 0) in 0.06s, then springs back. Lift and sink are carried by the `translate` property so they never collide with Motion's transforms, and the lift exists only where hover and a fine pointer both exist, so a tapped slab on touch never stays raised. Small controls scale the system down: 4px resting shadows instead of 6px.

#### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Slab rest** (`box-shadow: 6px 6px 0 var(--b-ink)`): task slabs, bento cards, the composer, state cards, toasts and the add and retry buttons.
- **Control rest** (`box-shadow: 4px 4px 0 var(--b-ink)`): icon buttons, the theme toggle, ES/EN, skin and filter groups, footer links, the logo tile, the pending badge, the completion stamp, the error balloon and icon tiles.
- **Lift** (`translate: -4px -4px; box-shadow: 10px 10px 0 var(--b-ink)`, hover with `(hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)` only): every pressable slab and control.
- **Sink** (`translate: 6px 6px; box-shadow: 0 0 0 var(--b-ink)`, 0.06s, on :active): every pressable slab and control.
- **Sticker drop** (`filter: drop-shadow(5px 5px 0 var(--b-ink))`): the SVG stickers, following their silhouettes.

#### Named Rules
**The Zero-Blur Rule.** Every Brutal shadow is a Nightfall offset with no blur. Depth never softens.

**The Press Rule.** Hover lifts (-4px, shadow 10px), press sinks (+6px, shadow 0), both on the user-pinned overshoot curve cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.56, 0.64, 1) over 0.22s, carried by `translate`, with hover lift gated to hover-capable fine pointers.

### Classic

The original soft layering: translucent white cards with a hairline border and an 8px backdrop blur over a canvas carrying a faint 44px grid (masked to a top ellipse) and large blurred colour halos. Shadows are soft and tinted: barely-there zinc under cards, violet glows under the accent.

#### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Card hush** (`box-shadow: 0 1px 3px 0 rgb(24 24 27 / 0.03), 0 1px 2px -1px rgb(24 24 27 / 0.03)`): pending task cards; done cards drop it and fade to 40% white.
- **Composer focus glow** (`box-shadow: 0 10px 15px -3px rgb(139 92 246 / 0.1), 0 4px 6px -4px rgb(139 92 246 / 0.1)`): the composer while focused; at rest it carries a zinc 5% hush.
- **Violet button glow** (`box-shadow: 0 4px 6px -1px rgb(139 92 246 / 0.25), 0 2px 4px -2px rgb(139 92 246 / 0.25)`): the add button; the logo tile carries a larger 30% violet glow.
- **Halos** (`filter: blur(64px)`): the violet/indigo/sky ellipse at the top (breathing over 16s) and the fuchsia disc at the right (drifting over 20s).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** (all skins) keep one behaviour layer: a skin changes how the board looks, moves and sounds, never what it does or what it exposes to assistive tech; copy may take the skin's voice (Comic's own empty-state title, punctuation stripped from Brutal and Classic greetings).
- **Do** (all skins) switch the whole skin at once through `data-skin`, including page ground, body face, selection, caret, focus ring, scrollbar and theme colour, and set it before first paint.
- **Do** (all skins) build every new surface in all three skins, each in its own grammar.
- **Do** (Comic) build every raised surface as an inked panel: paper, 3px ink border, stepped extrusion (default 5px depth) and a 6px halftone cast.
- **Do** (Comic) express state through depth: 8px on hover, 7px on focus, 2px when done, 3px for small controls.
- **Do** (Comic) time scripted motion on twos with stepped easing (floor(p * n) / n, or CSS steps()), 4-13 frames; keep springs for direct hover and tap response.
- **Do** (Comic) set lettering (Bangers with ink stroke, ink extrusion and offset cyan/magenta plates) only on shouted display text: greetings, SFX, the logotype.
- **Do** (Comic) put fixed ink (#14101f) on Process Yellow in both themes.
- **Do** (Comic) give panels a slight, stable tilt (within about plus or minus 0.7deg for list items, up to 2deg for single features).
- **Do** (Comic) draw decoration as CSS gradients and inline SVG; disable every decorative motion under prefers-reduced-motion.
- **Do** (Brutal) build raised surfaces as slabs: warm white, 4px Nightfall border, 14px radius, 6px zero-blur Nightfall shadow.
- **Do** (Brutal) scale small controls down to 3px or 2px borders and 4px resting shadows.
- **Do** (Brutal) make slabs and controls pressable with the Press Rule: lift -4px with a 10px shadow on hover-capable fine pointers, sink +6px to shadow 0 on press, on cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.56, 0.64, 1) over 0.22s, using `translate`.
- **Do** (Brutal) keep spring physics for entrances, plates, stamps and stickers, and stop the marquee and the cursor under reduced motion.
- **Do** (Brutal) set Nightfall (#351c4d) on Heatwave and Liquid Sunset and warm white on Paradise, in both themes.
- **Do** (Brutal) render stickers only from 1024px and the custom cursor only for fine pointers.
- **Do** (Classic) keep Classic faithful to the original; change it only for accessibility or fit.

### Don't:
- **Don't** (all skins) mix skins: no inked panels or Bangers outside Comic, no slabs or Nightfall offsets outside Brutal, no translucent blur cards or violet gradients outside Classic.
- **Don't** (all skins) fork behaviour per skin: tasks, filters, validation, shortcuts and accessible names stay identical across all three.
- **Don't** (Comic) use blurred shadows, glassy blur, or soft floating cards; depth is printed.
- **Don't** (Comic) use violet-to-pink gradients or any gradient other than halftone dots, speed lines and the sky masks.
- **Don't** (Comic) set task titles or any user content in Bangers.
- **Don't** (Comic) introduce characters, logos or franchise imagery; the reference is art style only.
- **Don't** (Comic) add a hue outside cyan, magenta, yellow, ink and paper (danger and the status green excepted).
- **Don't** (Comic) round panels beyond 4px; a pill or a 12px card breaks the cut-paper silhouette.
- **Don't** (Brutal) use blur, soft shadows or gradient fills; every shadow is a 0-blur Nightfall offset.
- **Don't** (Brutal) leave hover lift active on touch, or carry lift and sink on `transform`, which Motion owns for entrances.
- **Don't** (Brutal) add a hue outside Ponche Tropical (Nightfall, Tropical Pink, warm white, Liquid Sunset, Heatwave, Paradise and their night tints), the online mint excepted.
- **Don't** (Classic) carry its violet gradients, blur halos or 👋 into other skins or new surfaces; they exist because the user asked for the original restored.
- **Don't** (Classic) move the date back into an eyebrow above the greeting.
